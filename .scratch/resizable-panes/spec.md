# Resizable reader panes

Allow readers to allocate more width to either PDF without leaving the comparison workspace.

## Acceptance criteria

- The divider can resize the two panes between 25% and 75%.
- Left and right arrow keys adjust the split in 5% steps.
- The divider exposes its current value to assistive technology.
- The split ratio is restored from and written to the existing session snapshot.
- The stacked narrow-window layout remains usable.
